Anti-Fingerprint Noise Detector

Anti-Fingerprinting Noise Injection (Farbling / Perturbation): Evaluates whether your browser actively injects mathematical pseudo-random noise into Canvas pixel buffers and Audio frequency samples to spoil cross-site tracking fingerprints.

How It Works: Sentinel renders identical complex multi-layer canvas graphics and audio wave frequencies in two back-to-back micro-tasks:
  • Deterministic (Vulnerable): Pass 1 hash exactly equals Pass 2 hash (100% reproducible cross-site tracking fingerprint).
  • Noise Injection (Shield Active): Pass 1 hash differs from Pass 2 hash. Proves active browser defense (e.g., Brave Farbling, Firefox privacy.resistFingerprinting, Safari Advanced Tracking Protection, or Tor Browser).

Security Header Observatory

Security Header Observatory: Live Mozilla Observatory-style cryptographic policy auditor. Grades HTTP defense headers protecting clients against XSS, clickjacking, hardware sensor exfiltration, and SSL downgrade.

Grading Standard:
  • HSTS (Strict-Transport-Security): Max-age >= 31536000 with subdomains and preload enforcement.

  • Permissions-Policy: Disables unauthorized peripheral access (camera, microphone, geolocation, FLoC).
  • Frame & Sniff Defenses: X-Frame-Options: DENY and X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff.
